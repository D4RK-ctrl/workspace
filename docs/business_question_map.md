# Business question map

"Where should FlashEats operations focus to reduce late deliveries,
and is the available data trustworthy enough to identify the responsible
workflow stage?"

Each authority below is proposed pending owner confirmation. Source paths and measured limitations are detailed in `source_audit.md`.

| Decision | Question | Required information | Fields | Source | Authority | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prioritize late-delivery reduction | How frequent and severe are late completed deliveries? | Original promise, observed completion, eligibility | `order_id`, `final_status`, `promised_eta`, `actual_delivery_at` | SQL `orders` | Order platform for recorded promise/lifecycle | >0 versus >10 threshold, refund exclusions, missing completion and chronology policy unresolved |
| Target workflow stage | Does time accumulate before pickup or after pickup? | Comparable lifecycle intervals | `created_at`, `pickup_at`, `actual_delivery_at` | SQL `orders`; telemetry `type`, `timestamp` as separate evidence | Order lifecycle; telemetry owns its recorded milestones | Before-pickup interval combines preparation, assignment, travel and waiting; restaurant arrival absent |
| Hold restaurants accountable | Was food ready while the driver waited? | Actual readiness and restaurant arrival | Available only: restaurant `status`, `last_updated_at`; order `pickup_at`; telemetry `type`, `timestamp` | Restaurant-status CSV, orders, driver JSON | Reported status is not a verified readiness event | No observed arrival; update time need not equal readiness time; cannot assign responsibility |
| Improve dispatch | Are assignment changes associated with late orders? | Assignment history and order outcome | `driver_id`, `original_driver_id`, `assigned_at`, `reassigned_at`, `current_delivery_eta`, `estimated_pickup_at`, `dispatch_status`; SQL promise/completion | Dispatch API and orders | Dispatch assignment/estimate versus order original promise | Only a snapshot, no full change history; 95 driver mismatches need temporal interpretation |
| Focus on operating conditions | Which conditions coincide with delay? | Cohort-specific delays, traffic/weather/distance | `traffic_bucket`, `weather_bucket`, `distance_km_estimate`, `city`, `created_at` | SQL orders | Context recorded by order platform; measurement method unknown | Conflicting duplicate traffic values, estimated distance and confounding; correlations cannot identify cause |
| Reduce customer friction | Are late orders associated with repeated ETA checks or support opening? | Action counts/flags and outcome | `action_id`, `order_id`, `action_type`, `action_at`; order timestamps | Customer app actions and orders | App behavior versus lifecycle outcomes | Action tracking coverage unknown; actions after completion must be separated; no action does not prove no frustration |
| Reconcile customer experience | Do complaints correspond to recorded lateness? | Distinct complaint orders linked to lifecycle | `ticket_id`, `order_id`, `category`, ticket `created_at`, order promise/completion | Support CSV and orders | Support owns complaint; orders own lifecycle record | Missing links, duplicates, category variants, complaint-selection bias |
| Evaluate rescue actions | What late rate is observed with and without an intervention? | Intervention type/time, eligible outcomes, comparison cohorts | `intervention_id`, `intervention_type`, `intervention_at`, `initiated_by`, `reason`; SQL timestamps/status | Intervention CSV and orders | Operations action log, outcome from order system | Severe orders attract interventions; post-delivery actions and unavailable cancellation time; no causal treatment effect |
| Decide whether metrics are publishable | Are sources sufficiently complete and consistent? | Unique grains, mappings, parseability, retrieval evidence, agreed contract | Source keys/FKs/timestamps; API `page`, `page_size`, `total_records`, `has_more`, `data`; metric-definition document | All selected sources | Contract approved by business owner; retrieval demonstrated by pipeline | No canonical KPI owner, no complete source SLAs, unresolved source conflicts |

Proposed first use: identify descriptive cohorts and broad lifecycle intervals, paired with validation coverage. Do not label order-to-pickup as kitchen preparation time, replace the original promise with a newer dispatch ETA, turn a GPS estimate into an observed arrival, or describe intervention correlations as effectiveness.

Use SQL orders, app actions and intervention CSVs for the minimal workflow model. Add dispatch through its real API retrieval path and use support/restaurant/driver sources for targeted reconciliation. Treat compiled events, mixed interactions and precomputed outcomes as comparison evidence until their lineage is clarified; combining every source would duplicate business events.
